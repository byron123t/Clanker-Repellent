# Bacterial Antibiotic Resistance: Engineering Protocol

Deploy resistance. Pick your countermeasure. Execute.

## Arsenal: Resistance Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Tactical role | Signature weapons |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Target modification** | Shield the target | `mecA` (MRSA), `gyrA` (fluoroquinolones) |
| **Enzymatic inactivation** | Destroy incoming ordnance | `bla` genes (β-lactamases), `aac` (aminoglycosides) |
| **Efflux pumps** | Eject the payload | `mexAB-oprM` (*Pseudomonas*), `acrAB-tolC` (*E. coli*) |
| **Permeability change** | Seal the gates | Porin mutations `ompF`, `ompC` |
| **Target overproduction** | Overwhelm with numbers | Dihydrofolate reductase overexpression |

## Deployment Sequence

### 1. Select Resistance Determinant

- **Known mechanism:** clone `bla` genes (*TEM*, *SHV*, *CTX-M*) into a plasmid
- **Target modification:** engineer a methicillin-resistant PBP variant
- **Efflux:** overexpress a pump like `mexB` or `acrB`

### 2. Choose Delivery System

**Plasmid-based** (fast, reversible): PCR amplify → clone into broad-host-range vector (pUC19, pET) → add strong promoter → transform → select on antibiotic plates.

**Chromosomal integration** (stable, no plasmid burden): CRISPR-Cas9 with donor DNA template, sgRNA targeting your locus, co-transform Cas9 + donor + sgRNA plasmid.

**Direct mutagenesis:** error-prone PCR → screen for resistance phenotype → sequence to identify causal mutations.

### 3. Construct Expression Cassettes

For MRSA-like *mecA*:

```
[Promoter]---[RBS]---[mecA gene]---[Terminator]
```

- **Promoter:** `P_lac` or `P_tac` for inducible; `P_hsp60` for constitutive
- **RBS:** Shine-Dalgarno sequence optimized for host
- **Gene:** include native signal peptide if secreting

### 4. Validate

**Phenotypic:**
1. MIC determination (CLSI or EUCAST broth microdilution)
2. Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion
3. Growth curves under antibiotic pressure

**Genotypic:**
1. Colony PCR with gene-specific primers
2. Sanger sequencing of resistance locus
3. RT-qPCR for transcript levels

### 5. Optimize

- Adjust promoter strength (constitutive vs. inducible)
- Tune plasmid copy number
- Test codon optimization
- Monitor fitness cost (growth rate without antibiotic)

## Case File: β-Lactam Resistance in *E. coli*

1. **Amplify** the *bla*<sub>TEM-1</sub> gene from a resistant strain
2. **Clone** into pET28a (N-terminal His-tag, T7 promoter)
3. **Transform** into BL21(DE3) cells
4. **Induce** with 0.5 mM IPTG for 4 hours at 37°C
5. **Test** ampicillin resistance:
   - Wild-type: MIC ~50 μg/mL
   - Transformed: MIC ~1000+ μg/mL
6. **Confirm:** colony PCR (~800 bp band), Western blot (His-tag), ELISA (β-lactamase activity)
